5 FACTS ABOUT OTTERS

Densest fur in the animal kingdom

Sea otters do not have a layer of fat (blubber) to keep them warm. Instead, they rely on the densest fur of any animal, featuring up to 1 million hair follicles per square inch that trap an insulating layer of air

They use tools

Otters are among the few non-human mammals that use tools. They often float on their backs and use a rock as a hammer or anvil to crack open hard-shelled prey like clams, mussels, and sea urchins.

Armpit pockets

Sea otters have a loose patch of skin under each forearm that acts like a pocket. They use these pockets to store their favorite rocks or keep food safe while diving.

They hold hands while sleeping

When resting or sleeping in the water, sea otters form groups called rafts. They often hold hands or entangle themselves in giant kelp so they do not drift away from each other or out to sea.

High-calorie diet

Because they burn so much energy staying warm in cold water, otters have a massive metabolism and must consume 25% of their body weight in food every single day.
